# BabyCities — The app for parents on the go

BabyCities is a community-driven app that helps parents find baby-friendly places before they head out — so every outing feels a little less like guesswork.

## What is BabyCities?

Going out with a baby means thinking ahead: Is there a changing table? Can the stroller get through? Is there somewhere quiet to nurse?

BabyCities puts that practical knowledge at your fingertips, sourced from parents who've actually been there. Explore cafes, restaurants, parks and everyday spots near you — or plan ahead before you travel somewhere new — with the filters that actually matter to families.

## Baby filters

BabyCities lets you search by the small but essential details that make outings easier:

- 🔄 **Changing table** — spot places where diaper changes are properly set up
- 🪑 **High chair** — find cafes and restaurants where your baby joins the table comfortably
- 🍼 **Microwave** — check where warming a bottle or meal is possible before you arrive
- 🚼 **Stroller-friendly** — filter for places that stay practical when you arrive with a buggy
- 🤱 **Nursing area** — find spots where feeding breaks feel calmer and more comfortable
- 🛝 **Playground / Swing** — discover spots with a play area when toddlers need to move and reset

## The BabyCities community

BabyCities grows with every parent who contributes. You can rate places, leave practical reviews, add spots that aren't on the map yet, and save your favourite family-friendly locations for easy access later.

The more parents share, the more reliable and useful the app becomes for everyone.
